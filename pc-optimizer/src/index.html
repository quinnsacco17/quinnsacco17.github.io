Calibrate to your real machine

Run any game, note the average fps (use the in-game counter, Steam overlay, or MSI Afterburner over 1-2 minutes of normal play), then enter it here. The model rescales GPU and CPU performance to match. One measurement gets most of the gain; three different games is best.

Estimates use published benchmark averages. Uncalibrated error is about ±15%; calibrated about ±7%. Patches, drivers, scene, and thermals move real numbers.
